Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Our Privacy Promise: We respect your privacy. We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to anyone. We do NOT use third-party advertising tracking networks or data brokers. We only collect the absolute minimum data required to operate the game, save your account progress, and connect you with other players in multiplayer.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the InduFront game service, website, and related multiplayer servers ("Service"). By creating an account or accessing the Service, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. This document should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

2. What We Collect and Why (Only What Is Strictly Needed to Play)

We follow the principle of strict data minimization. We only process data necessary to provide a functioning multiplayer gaming service:

2.1 Account & Identity Data

  • Username & Email: Used exclusively to identify your account, enable login, and prevent duplicate accounts.
  • Password: Stored securely as an irreversible cryptographic hash (using modern salt + scrypt hashing). We can never see or read your actual password.
  • Google Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive only your Google account ID and verified email to link your login. We do not access contacts, files, or any other Google data.

2.2 Gameplay & Multiplayer Data

  • In-Game Stats & Level: Your public Player ID, match records, achievements, level, and selected cosmetics to render the game world and allow progression.
  • Friend Lists & Social Features: Friend connections and pending requests that you initiate so you can invite and play with friends.
  • Match Actions & Chat: Real-time in-game troop movements, chat messages, and commands transmitted over WebSockets during a match so that all players in the lobby stay synchronized.

2.3 Technical & Security Data

  • IP Addresses: Used ephemerally to establish WebSocket connections, route game traffic to game workers, and defend against malicious attacks (such as DDoS or rate-limit violations).
  • Session Tokens: Secure cryptographic JWT tokens issued to keep you authenticated during your session.

3. What We Explicitly DO NOT Do

Practice Our Policy
Selling Personal Data Never. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.
Third-Party Ad Networks None. We do not run third-party advertising networks, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling.
Data Profiling & Brokers None. We do not share your information with data brokers or credit bureaus.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use tracking or marketing cookies. We only use strictly necessary technical storage:

  • Essential Authentication Cookie: An HTTP-only, secure session cookie (refresh_token) to keep you logged in to your game account without re-entering credentials every visit.
  • Browser Local Storage: Used locally on your device to remember client-side preferences (such as audio volume, graphics settings, keybindings, and theme preferences).

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Providers

We only share data with infrastructure providers strictly needed to host and run the game:

  • Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure: Hosting providers (such as Render) that run our game servers, databases, and secure HTTPS/WSS network connections.
  • OAuth Providers (Optional): Google Identity Services, only if you explicitly choose to sign in via Google.
  • Legal Compliance: We will only disclose information if legally compelled by a binding court order or subpoena from authorized law enforcement authorities.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have full control over your data:

  • Instant Account Deletion: You can delete your account at any time directly through the in-game account settings or via our API (DELETE /users/@me). Once deleted, your account, login credentials, and friend links are permanently erased.
  • Server Logs: Temporary connection logs and IP rate-limiting entries are automatically rotated and discarded.

7. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Regardless of where you reside, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: View the data associated with your profile at any time in your in-game profile.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your username or password whenever you wish.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account and all associated personal data with one click.
  • Right to Object: Because we do not sell data or run marketing trackers, you do not need to opt out of tracking networks.
