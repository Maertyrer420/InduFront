Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Our Privacy Commitment: We respect your privacy. We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to any third parties or advertising networks. We collect strictly and exclusively what is technically necessary to operate an online multiplayer game in your browser.

This Privacy Policy applies to the InduFront game service, website, and multiplayer servers ("Service"). InduFront is an independent fork and project focused on strategic RTS gameplay.

1. Data Minimization Principles

We adhere to strict data minimization:

  • No Advertising Networks: We do not run third-party advertising scripts, retargeting pixels, behavioral profiling, or tracking cookies.
  • No Financial Data: We do not process payments, credit cards, or banking information. The game is free to play.
  • No Third-Party Data Brokers: Your data is never sold, traded, or shared with data aggregators.

2. What Information We Collect & Why

To enable browser multiplayer gameplay, we process only the following essential technical data:

A. Account & Profile Information (Optional)

  • Username: The display name you choose to identify yourself in game lobbies and matches.
  • Email Address: Used solely as your account login identifier and for password recovery. Never shared or used for unsolicited marketing.
  • Password Hash: Stored securely using standard cryptographic hashing (scrypt with unique salts). We never store plaintext passwords.

B. Gameplay & Multiplayer State

  • In-game Statistics: Match outcomes, match duration, rankings, and friendly leaderboard progress.
  • Lobby State: Game room configurations, player readiness status, and active game coordinates.

C. Technical & Security Logs

  • IP Address: Temporarily processed for low-latency WebSocket communication between your browser and our game server, and to protect our infrastructure against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and automated abuse.
  • Session Tokens: Secure cryptographic tokens (JSON Web Tokens / signed session cookies) stored locally in your browser to keep you logged in between visits.

3. How We Store Your Data

Your account information is stored securely on our server backend. Local game settings (such as volume preferences, visual settings, and active session tokens) are stored directly on your own device using standard browser localStorage.

4. Account Deletion & Your Control (GDPR / CCPA)

You have complete autonomy over your personal data at all times:

  • Immediate Self-Service Deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time directly through the in-game Account settings or via our API endpoint (DELETE /users/@me).
  • Permanent Erasure: When deleted, your account record, email, and authentication tokens are immediately and irreversibly purged from our active databases.
  • Export & Access: You can view all information associated with your account directly in your profile view.

5. Third-Party Services

InduFront connects only to the necessary infrastructure required to deliver game assets and multiplayer connectivity:

  • Game Hosting: Our servers are hosted on modern cloud infrastructure (e.g. Render) to serve game files and manage live WebSocket matches.
  • Optional Google Sign-In: If you explicitly choose to sign in via Google OAuth, Google authenticates your identity and provides your verified email.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy as InduFront evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
